## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Ensure selecting an AuthProvider changes the authentication mechanism without changing provider-neutral gateway authorization semantics.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 AuthProvider manifests expose the provider-neutral assurance evidence and AuthAssurancePolicy compatibility they can satisfy.
- [ ] #2 Gateway handoff authorization consumes provider-neutral assurance outcomes only and does not branch on raw provider ids, provider-native method names, amr, acr, WebAuthn flags, or delegated-provider stage names.
- [ ] #3 Missing, ambiguous, or provider-specific valid auth evidence degrades to the lowest safe tier or is refused with stable diagnostics.
- [ ] #4 A fake auth provider cannot satisfy phishing-resistant policy without the required user-verification, recipient-binding, and replay-resistant evidence.
<!-- AC:END -->
